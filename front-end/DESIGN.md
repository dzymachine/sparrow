## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for women undergoing life-altering transitions—specifically separation, divorce, and the pursuit of autonomous financial clarity. The emotional atmosphere must balance deep calm, safety, and quiet resolve with editorial sophistication. Users are often experiencing cognitive overload, anxiety, and vulnerability; the UI must never scream, startle, or demand. Instead, it provides sanctuary, steady agency, and gradual empowerment.

Drawing inspiration from high-end editorial finance and literary lifestyle journals, the design style merges **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Organic Tactility**. Content flows with breathing room, deliberate typography, and subtle natural textures. Visual metaphors lean exclusively into avian symbolism—light wings catching wind, nesting structures, stepping stones, and steady perches.

To ensure safety and emotional ease:
- **Strictly No Human Faces:** Avoid portraits or photographic emotional prompts that impose prescriptive sentimentality or trigger grief.
- **Strictly No Marital Symbols:** Never use imagery of wedding bands, fractured rings, severed knots, or broken home tropes.
- **Zero Alarming Signifiers:** Forbid sharp crimson/red errors, stark true blacks (#000000), harsh flashing banners, or abrasive modal alerts.
- **Constant Discreet Sanctuary:** A permanent, highly accessible, yet visually discreet "Quick Safe Exit" utility must always sit anchored in the viewport.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an earthy, dawn-lit landscape—soft cream, misted foliage, down feathers, and morning sky. It rejects clinical fintech aesthetics and avoids conventional transactional harshness.

- **Background & Canvas:** Soft Linen Cream (`#FAF7F2`) serves as the foundational backdrop, diffusing eye fatigue and replacing stark white with natural warmth. Secondary canvas layers use Pearl Oat (`#F4EFEA`) and Nest Card (`#EDE6DD`).
- **Primary Tone (Deep Forest Sage `#4F5E47`):** Used for primary actions, critical navigation elements, headings requiring authoritative clarity, and high-contrast accessible text.
- **Secondary Tone (Soft Meadow Sage `#8A9A7B`):** Employed for steady supporting elements, inactive progress perches, soft iconography, and gentle active state foundations.
- **Tertiary Accent (Feather Brown / Sand `#A07E5F`):** Used selectively for active milestone nodes, key numerical insights, discovery tags, and subtle navigational indicators.
- **Gentle Support (Muted Dawn Rose `#D9B3AC`):** Applied exclusively for warm confirmations, reassuring notices, soft highlights, and support hotline accents.
- **Soft Neutral Text & Borders:** Primary text reads as Deep Muted Charcoal Slate (`#2C332A`), secondary body as Earth Umber (`#5F635E`), and structural borders use faint warm ochre tints (`#E2DAD0`). Critical warnings use a subdued Warm Terracotta Ochre (`#A86851`), never jarring red.

## Typography

The typography pairs the literary, reflective grace of an authoritative transitional serif (`Newsreader`) with the pristine clarity and structural balance of `Inter`. 

- **Headlines & Meditative Prose:** Handled by Newsreader. Used in standard regular weight (400) to convey grounded confidence without visual aggression. Quotes, reflection cues, and chapter titles utilize italic variants to foster a supportive, conversational cadence.
- **Body & Numerical Information:** Handled by Inter with an intentional baseline size of 18px (`body-lg`) and a relaxed line height of 1.6+ (`30px`) for high legibility, reducing mental fatigue when processing financial documents, legal terms, or asset inventories.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Inter at medium and semi-bold weights with subtle tracking to maintain immediate scan-ability across badges, steps, and balance readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an editorial fluid-grid system structured around generous negative space. Rather than crowding data into dense dashboards, modules are grouped into spacious chapters and contemplative reading lanes.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop operates on a centered 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1160px`. Tablet transitions into an 8-column layout (`gutter: 1.25rem`), and mobile condenses to a 4-column column fluid flow (`gutter: 1rem`).
- **Reading Comfort:** Text content lanes and reflective discovery forms are restricted to an optimal reading width of `680px` (spanning 7-8 columns on desktop) to prevent eye fatigue.
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:** Vertical cadence relies heavily on `space-lg` (`2rem`) and `space-xl` (`3.5rem`) between thematic groupings, instilling patience and visual calm as the user steps through asset checklists and independence plans.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews dramatic, heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Soft Ambient Diffusion**. Depth is communicated through warm tonal shifts and delicate contours that mimic smooth river stones or lightweight paper resting upon linen.

- **Base Surfaces:** Foundation rests upon `#FAF7F2`. Elevated surfaces (cards, asset drawers, pathway markers) utilize flat `#FFFFFF` or warm sand `#F4EFEA` accompanied by a micro-border of `1px solid rgba(79, 94, 71, 0.08)`.
- **Soft Ambient Elevation:** When an element requires floating emphasis (e.g., interactive drawers, active perch steps, or flyout guides), apply an extra-diffused, earthy-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px -4px rgba(79, 94, 71, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(79, 94, 71, 0.04)`.
- **Active Focus & Hover:** Instead of high elevation spikes, interactive states shift smoothly via a warm tonal fill change and a subtle feather-brown outline (`rgba(160, 126, 95, 0.3)`).
- **Persistent Header:** Rests with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over a semi-translucent `#FAF7F2` surface (92% opacity), bordered below by a 1px soft hairline (`rgba(79, 94, 71, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is organic, reassuring, and gentle. Sharp 90-degree corners are completely eliminated to prevent harsh, clinical associations. 

- **Primary Geometry:** Base cards, modules, and form groups adopt `roundedness: 2` (base border-radius of `0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px` on container-level cards).
- **Pill Badges & Safe Controls:** Floating utility indicators, Quick Safe Exit buttons, step tags, and status badges take full pill curvature (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), evoking smooth pebble contours and aerodynamic softness.
- **Flight & Path Lines:** Milestone pathways and divider lines utilize soft 1.5px to 2px rounded strokes with subtle dashed intervals to evoke guided flight tracks.

## Components

### Quick Safe Exit
- Always pinned to the top-right header and duplicated in a sticky mobile utility strip.
- Styled as an understated, high-contrast pill button (`#4F5E47` border, crisp neutral label, accompanied by an instantaneous redirect and browser history purge shortcut). Does not use alarm colors, allowing discreet safety without drawing suspicion.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button:** Deep Forest Sage (`#4F5E47`) background with soft linen text (`#FAF7F2`), rounded to `0.75rem` or full pill. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: shifts to `#3E4A38` with an ease-in-out transition.
- **Secondary / Rest Button:** Transparent surface with a 1.5px border of Soft Sage (`#8A9A7B`) and text in `#4F5E47`.
- **Tertiary / Perch Action:** Feather brown (`#A07E5F`) text with an animated subtle underline that gracefully glides forward on hover.

### Milestone Pathways & Perches
- Replaces generic progress bars with an interactive avian flight track: a thin, organic dotted line connecting circular "perch nodes."
- **Completed Nodes:** Soft Sage (`#8A9A7B`) with a miniature delicate wing glyph.
- **Active Node:** Highlighted in Warm Sand Brown (`#A07E5F`) with a soft glowing ambient ring.
- **Future Nodes:** Gentle Oat cream (`#EDE6DD`) with a fine hairline boundary.

### Cards & Asset Discovery Modules
- Set upon pure white or warm oat backgrounds with `1rem` rounded corners and a delicate `1px` border of `rgba(79, 94, 71, 0.08)`.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` / `2rem`). Contains quiet typography hierarchy, itemized document drop-zones, and soothing guidance tooltips.

### Form Inputs & Checklists
- Generous touch targets (minimum `52px` height) with `0.5rem` curvature.
- Default border: `1.5px solid #E2DAD0`. Focus state: transitions to Warm Sand (`#A07E5F`) with an aura halo of `rgba(160, 126, 95, 0.15)`. No harsh primary blues or black outlines.
- Checkboxes and radios utilize organic rounded-square and pebble geometries, filling with Deep Forest Sage upon selection.

### Calm Disclaimer Badges & Hotlines
- Pinned informational notes and support hotline cards utilize Muted Dawn Rose tints (`#FAF2F0`) paired with a deep umber text (`#5F635E`) and subtle blush accents (`#D9B3AC`).
- Icons depict calm motifs: protective nests, feathers, or flight trajectories—never hazard exclamation triangles or sirens.
