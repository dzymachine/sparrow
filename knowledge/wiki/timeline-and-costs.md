# California Divorce Timeline and Filing Costs

## Plain English
Even if you and your spouse agree on everything, a California divorce cannot be final in less than six months and one day after your spouse is served. If there are contested issues, the process takes longer.

## Key Rules & Estimates
- **Statutory Minimum Window:** 6 months and 1 day from the date of legal service on the respondent; cannot be shortened by court or agreement [S11].
- **Average Duration:** Contested divorces average 12 to 24 months depending on court backlogs and dispute complexity [S11].
- **Response Window:** Respondent has 30 calendar days to file Form FL-120 after service [S20].
- **Residency Requirements:** 6 months in California, 3 months in the county of filing [S12].
- **Court Filing Fees:** State base filing fee is $435 as of 2026 [S12].
- **Fee Waivers:** If you cannot afford filing fees, submit Forms FW-001 (Request for Fee Waiver) and FW-003 (Order on Fee Waiver) [S9].
