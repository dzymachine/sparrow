# Essential California Judicial Council Divorce Forms

| Plain Description | Form | When Required | Filed or Served | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asks the court to start the divorce | FL-100 Petition | Start of case | Filed with court, served on spouse | S6 |
| Tells your spouse a case has started | FL-110 Summons | Start of case | Filed, served | S6 |
| Information about where your children have lived | FL-105 (UCCJEA declaration) | Start of case, only if you have children with your spouse | Filed | S6 |
| Asks to skip court filing fees | FW-001 and FW-003 (fee waiver) | With filing, if you can't afford fees | Filed | S9 |
| Cover form for your financial disclosures | FL-140 Declaration of Disclosure | At filing or within 60 days | Served on spouse, not filed with court | S7 |
| List of what you own and owe | FL-142 Schedule of Assets and Debts | With FL-140 | Served on spouse, not filed with court | S7 |
| Your income and expenses, with proof of income for the past two months | FL-150 Income and Expense Declaration | With disclosures, and with any request for support or fees | Served, and filed with requests | S7, S8 |
| Tells the court disclosures were served | FL-141 | After serving disclosures | Filed | S7 |
| Asks the court for an order during the case (support, fees, property use) | FL-300 Request for Order | Any time after filing | Filed, served | S10 |
| Asks the court to make your spouse help pay your legal fees | FL-319 | With FL-300 when asking for fees | Filed, served | S3, S4 |
| Your personal statement explaining why you need fee help | FL-158 (or comparable declaration) | With FL-319 | Filed, served | S3 |

## Warning for Users and Agents
Missing a required form can sink a request. In one California appeal (*Sharples v. Sharples*), a fee motion was denied because Form FL-319 had not been filed [S13]. Always submit the complete form package for any court request.
