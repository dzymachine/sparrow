# Temporary Spousal Support (Help with Bills While Case Is Open)

## Plain English
A divorce can take six months or much longer, and living expenses don't wait. You can ask the judge to order your spouse to help pay your living costs while the case is open. The judge looks at how much you need and how much your spouse can afford.

## Key Statutory Facts
- **When to ask:** You can request temporary support as soon as the divorce petition is filed [S1].
- **Legal Basis:** California Family Code section 3600 empowers the court to order temporary support in any amount necessary based on need and ability to pay [S19].
- **How to Ask:** File a Request for Order (Form FL-300) accompanied by a current Income and Expense Declaration (Form FL-150) [S10].
- **Decision Process:** Orders are made at a hearing without a full trial, relying primarily on Form FL-150 declarations [S19].
- **Guideline Calculations:** Many California counties use local guideline formulas (e.g., Santa Clara guideline: ~40% of higher earner's net income minus 50% of lower earner's net income) [S18]. Note that county guidelines vary and are recommendations rather than guarantees.
- **Temporary vs. Permanent:** Temporary support (FC § 3600) maintains status quo during litigation. Post-divorce spousal support is determined separately under California Family Code section 4320 [S18].
