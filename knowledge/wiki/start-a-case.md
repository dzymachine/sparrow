# Starting a Divorce Case in California

## Plain English
Starting a divorce case requires preparing initial petition forms and submitting them to the Superior Court in your county.

## Steps & Required Forms
1. **Form FL-100 (Petition):** Specifies the legal grounds (irreconcilable differences) and requests for property division or support [S6].
2. **Form FL-110 (Summons):** Contains standard family law restraining orders (ATROS) restricting property transfers and child removal once served [S6].
3. **Form FL-105 (UCCJEA):** Required if minor children are involved to report custody jurisdiction [S6].
4. **Filing & Fee Waivers:** Pay court filing fee ($435) or file Form FW-001 / FW-003 for fee waiver [S9].
