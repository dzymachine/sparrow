# Legal Separation in California

## Overview
Legal separation divides community property, establishes spousal/child support, and determines custody orders without terminating the legal marital status.

## Key Differences from Divorce
- **No 6-Month Wait:** Does not require the 6-month statutory waiting period before orders are binding.
- **No Residency Requirement:** Can be filed immediately upon moving to California, then amended to divorce once residency requirements (6 months state / 3 months county) are met.
- **Marital Status Preserved:** Parties remain legally married and cannot remarry unless converted to dissolution.
