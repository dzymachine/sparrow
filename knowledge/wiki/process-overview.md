# The California Divorce Process (Simplified Standard Path)

## Plain-English Summary
There are six phases: get ready, start the case, share the money picture, get help while you wait, agree or go to court, and the divorce becomes final. The earliest a divorce can be final is six months and one day after your spouse is served [S11].

## Steps and Dependencies

1. **Check residency.** At least one spouse must have lived in California 6 months and in the filing county 3 months [S12]. Next: step 2.
2. **Gather documents.** Proof of income for the past two months and recent tax returns [S8]. Feeds steps 5 and 6.
3. **File the petition.** Petition (FL-100) and Summons (FL-110); add FL-105 if you have children with your spouse [S6]. Filing fee or fee waiver (see timeline-and-costs). Next: step 4. Temporary help (step 6) can be requested once the case is filed [S1].
4. **Serve your spouse.** Starts the six-month minimum clock [S11]. Your spouse has 30 days to respond [S20]. Next: steps 5 and 7; gates step 8.
5. **Share the money picture.** FL-140, FL-142, FL-150, FL-141, at filing or within 60 days [S6, S7]. The 60 days can be changed by written agreement or court order [S21]. Depends on step 2.
6. **Get help while you wait (optional).** Temporary support and help with legal fees. See temporary-support and attorney-fees. Depends on steps 2 and 5 (FL-150 is reused).
7. **Agree or go to court.** Settlement agreement, or a judge decides at trial. Contested cases average 12 to 24 months [S11; verify].
8. **Final judgment.** Earliest six months and one day after service; cannot be shortened [S11].

## Key Dependencies
- Documents (step 2) feed disclosures (step 5) and requests (step 6).
- Service (step 4) starts the clock that gates the judgment (step 8).
- FL-150 is shared between disclosures and requests.
- Children add forms at filing.

## Where People Get Stuck
Can't see the family accounts (step 2); intimidating forms and fees (step 3); incomplete disclosures (step 5); not knowing the requests in step 6 exist; the long wait in step 7.

## Scope Limits
Defaults when a spouse never responds, domestic violence restraining orders, summary dissolution details, county-specific rules.
