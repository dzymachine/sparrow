# Service & Response Requirements in California

## Personal Service Rule
Court papers (FL-100, FL-110) must be personally served on your spouse by an adult who is not a party to the case.

## Response Window
- The respondent spouse has **30 calendar days** from the date of service to file a Response (Form FL-120) [S20].
- Proof of Service (Form FL-115) must be filed with the court to verify service [S6].

## Automatic Restraining Orders (ATROS)
Upon service, standard restraining orders automatically take effect preventing both spouses from hiding assets, transferring property, or changing insurance beneficiaries.
