# California Financial Disparity & Divorce Context Statistics

## Educational Context & Research Findings
*(For educational literacy and platform background; not individualized legal predictions.)*

- **Income Loss for Women:** Women's household income drops by 41% to 50% following divorce in U.S. longitudinal studies, nearly double the drop experienced by men [S15, S23].
- **Breadwinner Dynamics:** Husbands are primary breadwinners in 55% of opposite-sex marriages, earn about equal in 29%, and wives earn more in 16% [S14].
- **Self-Representation Rate:** Approximately 70% to 80% of California family law cases involve at least one self-represented litigant [S16].
- **Civil Justice Gap:** Over 85% of low-income civil legal needs go unmet due to cost barriers [S17, S24].
- **Financial Barriers to Exit:** 28% of surveyed individuals reported remaining in an unhappy or controlling relationship longer than desired due to financial fear [S22].
