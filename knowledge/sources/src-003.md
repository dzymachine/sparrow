# Share Your Financial Information (Financial Disclosures)

Sharing your financial information with your spouse or domestic partner is a required step in every divorce or legal separation in California.

## Key Rules
- **Do NOT file financial documents with the court.** You only share them directly with your spouse, then file Form FL-141 to declare service.
- **Why share:** Helps both parties make fair decisions on money and property. Be honest and complete; hiding information can result in court sanctions or fee penalties.
- **Preliminary Disclosures Deadline:** 
  - Petitioner: within 60 days of filing Petition.
  - Respondent: within 60 days of filing Response.
- **Final Disclosures:** Can be completed later or waived using Form FL-144 (Stipulation and Waiver of Final Declaration of Disclosure).
