# Get a Divorce in California

In California, the legal word for divorce is **dissolution**. Dissolution is the legal process to end a marriage or domestic partnership.

## So, what's a divorce?
Divorce is a legal way to end a marriage or domestic partnership. After a divorce, you're no longer married. In your divorce case, the judge can make decisions about things like money, property, and children. You don't need your spouse's agreement to get a divorce. You can get divorced for any reason.

## Domestic Violence and Safety
Divorce can be stressful and unsafe if there is domestic violence or abuse. If you are in immediate danger, call 911. Free, confidential help is available 24/7 via the National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-SAFE).

## Options
1. **Divorce:** Most common option. One spouse files, the other responds.
2. **Divorce by Joint Petition:** Both spouses file together.
3. **Summary Dissolution:** Faster, simpler process for marriages under 5 years with minimal property/debt and no children.
4. **Legal Separation:** Court orders on property and support without ending marital status.
5. **Annulment:** Marriage declared invalid from the start.

## California Residency Requirements
To start a divorce case in California, one spouse must have lived in:
- California for the last 6 months, and
- Current California county for the last 3 months.
